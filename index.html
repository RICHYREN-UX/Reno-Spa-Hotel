About Reno Spa

A Century of British Hospitality

Reno Spa began life in 1897 as a grand private townhouse, built for a family of Victorian merchants who loved to entertain. In 1924 its doors opened to the public as a small, exclusive hotel, and a reputation for warm, unhurried service was born.

After a sensitive restoration in 2019, the hotel now blends its original Edwardian architecture (sweeping staircases, plasterwork ceilings and a glass-roofed Winter Garden) with contemporary comfort. Our 214 rooms and suites are individually designed, and our team of more than 300 colleagues live by one principle: true luxury is being remembered.

Set on a quiet, tree-lined street moments from Reno Gardens, we are perfectly placed for museums, shopping, theatre and the very best of Mombasa.

  • Elegant rooms & suites with Hyde Park views
  • Exceptional, personalised service
  • Prime central Mombasa location
